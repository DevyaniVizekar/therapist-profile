# 🌿 Dr. Maya Reynolds 

### 🧠 A calm, modern & human-centered therapy experience

**🔗 Live Demo**

👉 [**Visit the Live Website**](https://therapist-profile-three.vercel.app/)

<br>

A thoughtfully designed and fully responsive therapy practice website for **Dr. Maya Reynolds, PsyD**, a licensed clinical psychologist based in Santa Monica, California. The website combines a calm visual identity, clear content structure, responsive layouts, subtle animations, and an intuitive user experience to create a welcoming digital space for prospective therapy clients.

</div>

---

## ✨ About the Project

This website is designed around one simple goal:

> 🌱 **Make the first step toward therapy feel comfortable, clear, and approachable.**

The experience focuses on building trust through:

- 🧠 Clear and meaningful therapy information
- 🌿 Calm and reassuring visual design
- 💬 Human-centered content
- 🎯 Clear calls-to-action
- 📱 Fully responsive layouts
- ✨ Subtle and purposeful animations
- 🏡 A dedicated office experience
- ❓ Easy-to-access FAQs
- 🤝 A welcoming client journey

---

## 🎨 Design Approach

The visual direction uses a soft, natural palette combined with editorial typography to create a feeling of **calm, warmth, trust, and professionalism**.

### 🌱 Design Principles

- ✨ Minimal and uncluttered layouts
- 🫧 Generous whitespace
- 📝 Strong typography hierarchy
- 🌿 Soft natural colors
- 🖼️ Large editorial imagery
- 🔘 Clear and consistent buttons
- 📐 Balanced spacing and alignment
- 💚 Calm visual transitions
- 📱 Responsive-first layouts

The design intentionally avoids an overly clinical appearance and instead creates a space that feels **warm, personal, and approachable**.

## 👥 Who I Help

The website is structured around three primary audiences:

### 🌱 High-Achieving Adults

For thoughtful and self-aware adults who may appear capable on the outside while experiencing exhaustion, overthinking, or emotional pressure internally.

### 💼 Professionals, Entrepreneurs & Creatives

For people experiencing high internal pressure who want to move away from burnout and build a more sustainable way of living and working.

### 🧠 Adults Healing From Past Experiences

For adults navigating trauma, chronic stress, relationship patterns, or earlier experiences that continue to affect their confidence, safety, and connection.
